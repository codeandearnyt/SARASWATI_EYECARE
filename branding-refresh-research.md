# Complete Logo Refresh Research

The original Saraswati Eye Care Centre homepage publishes a combined eye-icon and wordmark asset at:

`https://saraswatieyecarecentre.com/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fsarsawati__logo_21.png&w=640&q=100`

This asset contains the clinic icon and the full Saraswati Eye Care Centre text, so it can replace the current separate mark image and adjacent text treatment in the public header and footer.

The optimized combined logo is 432 × 75 pixels. The original clinician portrait files contain transparent cutouts; they should be rendered with a clean white portrait card and no blend mode or decorative ring, rather than allowing transparent regions to appear dark against the purple section.
